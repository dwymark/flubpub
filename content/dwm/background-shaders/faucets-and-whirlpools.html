THE TREE AND THE FUNGUS

Lopsided coupling

You sense the world widely; it senses it narrowly, through a slot. You carry a private self across the years; it carries nothing, rebuilds each turn. Call these the sensorium asymmetry and the maintenance asymmetry. They are not deficits. They are how the parts divide the labor.
