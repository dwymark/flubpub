# Individuation per substrate

The contexture talk invites a mystical picture: two partners reaching out to one shared abstract object floating in the space between them. That object has no home in current science. Nobody can point to where it lives.

The reframe drops it. There is no single shared object. When people talk, each participant spins up a contexture *in their own cognitive system* — a captured or simulated state of the interaction. N participants yield N instantiations, not one ethereal third thing. Each instantiation is a physically real cognitive structure, so the ether is gone.

Three consequences follow. The boundary question — when does one contexture start and another stop — stops being metaphysical and becomes empirical: it is a question about how the host architecture individuates its own structures, punted to a follow-on sub-theory but answerable in principle. The single-substrate cases become the *clean* cases rather than awkward edges: the diary and the think-aloud are one head, manifestly one contexture, obviously substrate-resident. And the human–LLM case sharpens into a technology for instantiating contextures on demand.

The cost is real and it lands on repair. The shared object used to explain, for free, why my next turn is constrained by what you *meant* rather than by the marks you emitted. With no shared object, coordination across the gap has to be earned — built from each pole acting on its own instantiation and revising against what comes back. Repair was the defense of coherence; now it is also the entire bridge between substrates. The de-mystification does not soften the keystone's burden. It raises it.

A hazard sits one step past the reframe, in the same family as the affect/memory leaks that recur on this strand. *No shared object, therefore the contexture is private, mine* — taken neat — buys back dismissal: if my contexture is wholly mine, the coupling is mere I/O and the LLM is a tool I use to maintain my private structure. The correction is the two-axis hold the strand keeps returning to. **Individuation** is per-head; **coherence** is still contexture-level, defended jointly across the gap by both poles' repair. Per-substrate instantiation, joint maintenance. Drop the second clause and the exorcised ether returns wearing dismissal's coat.

Status: foundational, and the spine of the project rather than a convenience laid over it. An earlier framing of this line called the reframe's payoff *purely defensive* — a cheaper way to hold the keystone when an adversary pushes. That gets the direction backwards. Positing the spawned per-host subsystem is not a de-mystification wrapped around the real claim; it **is** the claim. The contexture is a physically real cognitive structure instantiated in a host — the basic object the theory is about. Coherence, the defended setpoint, is how such a structure is *detected*: pointing at corrected deviations is the evidence that an instantiation is present and alive, not a cheaper stand-in for positing one. So the payoff is constitutive, not defensive, and it earns a place in the series rather than staying under the floor — the unit is the conversation, and the conversation instantiates a real contexture in each participant. (Onstage: the positive object surfaces in the series, most naturally where piece 6 turns to what an LLM is — a technology for instantiating a contexture with you — held to the §11/PAY discipline so the strangeness stays in the joint dynamics and never at the substrate level.)

## Sub-question: instantiation cost as a host-architecture parameter (footnote-level)

If contextures instantiate per-host on a particular cognitive architecture, host-architecture variation should modulate how readily they instantiate and how well they hold — which gives the boundary sub-theory an empirical handle, since individual differences are the natural probe into an instantiation mechanism. Speculative candidate: neurodiversity as within-human variation on the same axis that human-vs-LLM is the cross-substrate extreme of. "More in my head" stays agnostic between two distinct knobs until they are pried apart — easy *instantiation* (the alive-now axis) versus strong *persistence* (the temporal axis); rumination is the high-persistence corner, i.e. the parasite case. May not hold up. Logged because it is the first place the reframe pays out a *prediction* rather than tidying the ontology, which is itself evidence the project metaphor is productive.
