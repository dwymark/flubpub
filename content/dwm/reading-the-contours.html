THE WORM THAT KNEW TWO ROADS

A staying-on-target thing

The plan does not live in a brain. It lives in the whole body, smeared across the cells, a goal the tissue keeps trying to reach. Thinking, he says, is not a brain thing. It is a staying-on-target thing. Things that hold a shape against the world are already a little bit minded.
