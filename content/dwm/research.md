## Research & Publications

**Ikema-Japanese Dictionary (contributing editor)**  
池間方言辞典  
[View on eScholarship](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2tk7q26d)  
Contributed to editing and produced the Japanese→Ikema reverse index. Currently working on an English translation of the dictionary when time permits.

**Proposal to the IPA (co-author)**  
_Proposal for superscript diacritics for prenasalization, preglottalization and preaspiration_  
[Read article (PDF)](https://linguistics.ucla.edu/people/keating/Keating_JIPA_diacritics_accepted_complete_Feb2019.pdf)  
Collaborative research project with Prof. Patricia Keating and Ryan Sharif, submitted to the International Phonetic Association.

**Undergraduate Thesis**  
_Towards a Boolean Intensional Semantics_  
[Poster Presentation, AAAS Annual Meeting (2017)](https://aaas.confex.com/aaas/2017/webprogram/Paper20079.html)  
My undergraduate honors thesis in linguistics at UCLA, exploring intensionality using a novel Boolean algebraic framework. Presented as a poster at the 2017 AAAS Annual Meeting in Boston.
