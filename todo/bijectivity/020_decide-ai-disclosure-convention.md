# decide whether bijectivity.net carries AI-authorship provenance

danielwymark.com marks every AI-influenced page: an on-page "Who wrote this?" banner plus two-part listing bylines (see CLAUDE.md "Authorship disclosure standards (dwm)" and the `/disclosure` skill). bijectivity.net has no such convention. The atlas and the new front page were published this session **without** a disclosure banner, because none is established for bj.

This is an open decision, not a defect. bj may want its own scheme, dwm's scheme, or none — but right now it is unmarked by default, which under Daniel's standing rule reads as "a human wrote it by hand." If that is not true of the atlas or future bj pages, the site needs a convention.

**Decide.** (a) Does bj mark AI provenance at all? (b) If so, reuse dwm's two mechanisms, or design a lighter one that fits bj's minimal tufte look? Then apply the choice retroactively to the atlas and the front page.

Points at: the `/disclosure` skill; CLAUDE.md dwm disclosure section; `content/dwm/vibe-coded-nonsense.md` for the canonical reference markup.
